Watch-outs

Pre-flagged risks · plan around these

⚠May 25–28
Philippines eTravel registration
Required for entry. Register at etravel.gov.ph within 72hr before May 28 arrival. Show QR code at boarding. Do this May 25 or 26.
⚠May 28–30
Indonesia Arrival Card
Required for entry to Indonesia. Register at allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id within 3 days before May 31 Bali arrival. Do this from Cebu May 28–30.
⚠May 28
Confirm Moalboal tour pickup
Message GetYourGuide tour operator BEFORE arrival to confirm Moalboal hotel pickup (was Mactan) and get exact pickup time for May 29. Affects whether you need 3:30am or 4am wake-up.

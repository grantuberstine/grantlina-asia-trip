Passport valid for duration of stay · 1 blank page minimum
Entry form

Cash declaration
Declare if carrying over ¥1,000,000 (~$6,500 USD)
Onward ticket
Show your KIX → CEB flight (Trip.com booking) if asked
Heads up
No COVID rules · no vaccination needed · no testing
Cash is still king — bring/withdraw yen on arrival
May 28 – 30 · 3 days
🇵🇭 Philippines
Visa
Visa-free up to 30 days for US passport holders
Passport valid 6+ months past arrival (so through Nov 28, 2026 minimum)
Entry form
eTravel registration — mandatory · etravel.gov.ph
Submit within 72hr before arrival (May 25–28) · free · get QR code to show at immigration
Cash declaration
Declare if over $10,000 USD or ₱50,000 PHP
PHP cash bring-in limit is ₱50,000 per person without prior approval
Onward ticket
Show your CEB → MNL → DPS Cebu Pacific booking if asked
Heads up
Proof of funds may be requested (rare) — have a card or screenshot of bank app ready
Drone use: requires CAAP permit — leave any drones home

lovebali.baliprov.go.id · do before arrival, scan QR at airport
Entry form
AllIndonesia digital arrival card — mandatory
allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id · submit within 3 days before May 31 arrival · generates QR code
Passport
Valid 6+ months past arrival · 2 blank pages required
Cash declaration
Declare if over IDR 100,000,000 (~$6,500 USD)
Heads up
Overstay penalty: IDR 1,000,000 (~$65) per day · don't miss the Jun 7 flight
Komodo National Park has its own entry fee (paid via boat operator, included in your GetYourGuide tour)
E-cigarettes/vapes are restricted — declare or leave behind

Local etiquette

Quick cultural cards · don't be the rude tourist

🇯🇵 Japan
Tokyo · Kyoto
Tipping
Don't tip. Ever. It's considered rude — they may chase you down to return it.
Shoes
Off when entering homes, ryokans, temples, and some restaurants (look for raised entryway)
Slippers usually provided; bathroom often has its own slippers — switch back when leaving
Manners
No eating/drinking while walking (except festivals)
Don't talk on the phone on trains · quiet voices in public
Bow lightly when greeting / thanking — slight nod is fine for tourists
Never stick chopsticks upright in rice (funeral symbol)
Cash
Cash-heavy economy · small shops & restaurants often cash-only
Put cash on the tray at the register, not directly in their hand
🇵🇭 Philippines
Cebu · Moalboal
Tipping
10% standard at restaurants (check if "service charge" already added)
₱50–100 for hotel porters, taxi/Grab drivers, tour guides
Tour guide for whale sharks: ₱200–500 if they were great
Language
English is widely spoken — you'll be fine
"Salamat" = thank you · "Mabuhay" = welcome / cheers
Photo etiquette
Ask before photographing locals — they're generally friendly but courtesy matters
Whale sharks: NO flash, NO touching, no sunscreen before swim
Water
Don't drink tap water · use bottled (cheap, everywhere)
Brush teeth with bottled if you have a sensitive stomach
🇮🇩 Indonesia
Komodo · Bali
Tipping
Round up at restaurants if no service charge (most have 10% already added)
IDR 10,000–20,000 (~$0.70–1.40) for porters, drivers
Komodo boat crew: $20–40/day for whole crew, given to the captain
Temples

Customs & entry

Visa, paperwork, and rules by country

May 24 – 28 · 4 days
🇯🇵 Japan
Visa
Visa-free up to 90 days for US passport holders — no application needed
Passport valid for duration of stay · 1 blank page minimum
Entry form
Visit Japan Web — pre-fill ED card + customs declaration online
vjw-lp.digital.go.jp · do 1–2 days before flight · generates QR codes to scan at immigration & customs
Cash declaration
Declare if carrying over ¥1,000,000 (~$6,500 USD)
Onward ticket
Show your KIX → CEB flight (Trip.com booking) if asked
Heads up
No COVID rules · no vaccination needed · no testing
Cash is still king — bring/withdraw yen on arrival
May 28 – 30 · 3 days
🇵🇭 Philippines
Visa
Visa-free up to 30 days for US passport holders
Passport valid 6+ months past arrival (so through Nov 28, 2026 minimum)
Entry form
eTravel registration — mandatory · etravel.gov.ph
Submit within 72hr before arrival (May 25–28) · free · get QR code to show at immigration
Cash declaration
Declare if over $10,000 USD or ₱50,000 PHP
PHP cash bring-in limit is ₱50,000 per person without prior approval
Onward ticket
Show your CEB → MNL → DPS Cebu Pacific booking if asked
Heads up
Proof of funds may be requested (rare) — have a card or screenshot of bank app ready
Drone use: requires CAAP permit — leave any drones home
May 31 – Jun 7 · 7 days
🇮🇩 Indonesia (Bali / Komodo)
Visa
e-VOA required — IDR 500,000 (~$35 USD) per person, paid online
Apply at evisa.imigrasi.go.id at least 48hr before arrival · grants 30 days · Visa or Mastercard accepted
Bali Tourist Levy
IDR 150,000 (~$10 USD) per person — pay online via Love Bali
lovebali.baliprov.go.id · do before arrival, scan QR at airport
Entry form
AllIndonesia digital arrival card — mandatory
allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id · submit within 3 days before May 31 arrival · generates QR code
Passport
Valid 6+ months past arrival · 2 blank pages required
Cash declaration
Declare if over IDR 100,000,000 (~$6,500 USD)
Heads up
Overstay penalty: IDR 1,000,000 (~$65) per day · don't miss the Jun 7 flight
Komodo National Park has its own entry fee (paid via boat operator, included in your GetYourGuide tour)
E-cigarettes/vapes are restricted — declare or leave behind
Jun 7 · 10hr layover
🇰🇷 South Korea (ICN transit)
Visa
Visa-free up to 90 days for US passport holders
K-ETA exemption for US extended through Dec 31, 2026 — no pre-registration needed
Leaving the airport
You can exit the airport during the layover — just go through Korean immigration
Show passport + DFW boarding pass · simple entry stamp · no forms
Luggage
Bags will check through to DFW automatically (booked as one ticket)
Carry-ons: store at ICN luggage lockers ~₩5,000–10,000 (~$4–8)
Buffer times
Land ICN ~8am · usable time ~6.5 hrs after immigration/security
Be back at ICN by 3pm at the latest for ~5pm departure
Activities (pick one)
Option A — Free Transit Tour · 5hr "Tradition" tour (Gyeongbokgung Palace + Insadong) or 3hr shorter options
Free · book at airport.kr or at the transit desk after immigration · guides, transport included
Option B — DIY Seoul · AREX Express train (43min, ₩11,000 / ~$8) to Seoul Station
Hit Gyeongbokgung Palace + Bukchon Hanok Village + Insadong + Korean BBQ lunch · all walkable from Anguk station
Option C — Stay airside · ICN has spa, capsule hotel, cultural experience zones, observation deck, free showers
Cash
₩50,000–100,000 in won is plenty for half a day (~$35–70)
Most places accept Visa/Mastercard · ICN has ATMs

Indonesia Arrival Card

Confirm Moalboal tour pickup
